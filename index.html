How to play

Your first dive

01 · The goalSink to 6,000 m

You start at the surface and sink through four real ocean zones. At 6,000 m a vast glowing heart rises from below. Touch it with at least 25 light and the whole ocean lights up. A dive takes about 8 to 10 minutes, and there is no game over: if your light runs low, help drifts toward you.

0–200 mSunlight zoneBright, with a coral reef. Light fades slowly.
200–1,000 mTwilight zoneDim blue. Glowing jellies. First shadows near 650 m.
1,000–4,000 mMidnight zoneDark. You only see what your light reaches.
4,000–6,000 mThe abyssDarkest. Vents send up warm embers.

02 · ControlsSwimming and pulsing

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag anywhere to swim. Your creature stays just above your fingertip so you can see it.
  • Quick tap to pulse light.
  • Pause is in the top corner. Sound is in the pause menu.

On a computer

  • Move the mouse to swim, or use ←↑→↓ or WASD.
  • Click or press Space to pulse.
  • P or Esc pauses, G opens the field guide, M mutes.

Your position sets your speed. Stay low on the screen to sink faster, or hang near the top to slow down and look around.

03 · Your lightKeep yourself glowing

The meter at the bottom of the screen shows your light, from 0 to 100. It fades over time, and faster the deeper you go. Your light is also how far you can see in the dark zones, so a bright creature sees more.

YouA small glowing jellyfish. You get bigger and brighter as your light rises.
Running lowBelow 10 light, the meter turns orange, you slow down, and extra motes appear near you.

04 · CollectWhat to gather

Light motes · +4Glowing specks that drift up from below. Swim through them. They float toward you when you are close.
JellyfishBrush past one and it flashes, releasing five motes. It recharges after a few seconds.
Vent embers · +2.5In the abyss, hot vents send up a steady stream of warm embers.
Super magnet · 12 sA pink orb. Pulls every mote on screen toward you.
Dive surge · 10 sA blue orb. You sink about twice as fast and swim faster.
Radiance · 10 sA gold orb. Your light stops fading and shadows flee from you.

Power orbs rise from below about every 30 to 45 seconds. Active powers show a countdown in the corner of the screen.

05 · AvoidShadows

Shadow eelsFrom about 650 m down. In the dark you mostly see two red eyes. While one touches you, you lose about 17 light a second, and the screen edges turn red.
PulseCosts 6 light. Stuns every shadow nearby for a few seconds and pushes it away. It also pulls nearby motes in.

06 · DiscoverMeeting animals

Twenty real species swim at the depths where they actually live, from whale sharks near the surface to bigfin squid in the abyss. An animal with a dotted ring is not in your field guide yet. Swim close to it and it is logged: its name appears above it and a card opens at the top of the screen.

Not yet foundThe dotted ring disappears once the animal is in your field guide.
The coral reefBetween about 20 and 180 m, reef walls line both sides. Look for the anemone with a clownfish.

07 · LearnReading the cards

Every 200 m you descend unlocks a depth log entry, a fact about the ocean tied to that depth. Every new animal brings a species card. Cards appear as a slim banner at the top of the screen, where nothing new swims in.

  1. 1The top line says what kind of card it is. A species card shows how many you have found and the depth range where the animal lives. A depth log card reads like Depth log · 1,200 m · Pressure: the depth you unlocked it at and its topic, such as Light, Life, Geology or Record.
  2. 2The title, with the scientific name in italics for species, or the entry number for depth facts.
  3. 3The fact itself. Figures marked "about" are rounded, because real measurements vary between sources.
  4. 4The timer bar shrinks until the card closes, after about 7 to 10 seconds.
  5. 5Close it early with the ×. If you swim up under the banner, it fades out of your way and its timer slows down.

Nothing is lost if you miss a card. Every fact and animal is saved in the Field guide, which also explains the five oceans and some notable seas.

08 · SavingPause, resume, restart

Your dive saves itself as you go. Leave from the pause menu, close the page, or switch apps, and you can Resume from the same depth on the start screen. Restart takes you back to the surface. Your field guide and fastest time stay saved in this browser across every dive.
